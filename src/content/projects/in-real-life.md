Twelve-year-old Jayden is pulled into a world of dares, loyalty and escalating risk when his friend Riley plans to burn down a local shop in what he sees as an act of moral justice. Caught between fear, friendship, and the question of what it means to be a man, Jayden must navigate a series of choices that test his loyalty, his conscience, and his sense of belonging.
