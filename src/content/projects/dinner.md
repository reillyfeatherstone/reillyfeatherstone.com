Dinner by Moira Buffini is a darkly satirical comedy about a high-society dinner party that descends into chaos. Hostess Paige throws a lavish yet twisted celebration for her husband Lars, a modern-day philosopher, serving a grotesque meal designed to humiliate him and unsettle their pretentious guests. As the night unfolds, with topics like murder, truth, and suicide on the menu, civility breaks down and egos are ruthlessly skewered.
