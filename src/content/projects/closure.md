**Closure** is a short drama following a young uni student who is struggling to deal with the death of his girlfriend. Realising it may be his fault, he falls into a depression, pushing the blame onto friends and family in order to deal with his loss. But to move on, he must accept the reality of the situation, he must find CLOSURE.

Set across one day, weeks after the accident, CLOSURE offers drama and struggle but reminds us that the people closest to us shouldn’t be forgotten.
