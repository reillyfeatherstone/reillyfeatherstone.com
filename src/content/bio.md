# Reilly Featherstone is an **actor**, **musician**, and **theatre maker** based in Cardiff.

He trained at the [Royal Welsh College of Music & Drama](https://www.rwcmd.ac.uk/), graduating with an MA in Acting in 2025. He has worked with companies including [Welsh National Theatre](https://www.welshnationaltheatre.com/), [Sherman Theatre](https://www.shermantheatre.co.uk/) and more. With a particular interest in new writing, Reilly has participated in a number of research and development projects, exploring new plays such as **_Uncle Christopher_** as part of **Simon Harris'** WNT Studio pilot. He went on to premiere the new Welsh plays _[In Real Life](/projects/in-real-life)_ and _[Salem](/projects/salem)_, the latter of which went on to the West End.

Alongside new writing, Reilly has a passion for classical theatre and the enduring relevance of these stories to contemporary audiences. His work has taken him from West Wales to London's West End, with performances at venues including the [Young Vic](https://www.youngvic.org/), [Savoy Theatre](https://www.thesavoytheatre.com/), **Richard Burton Theatre**, **The Caird**, [Chapter Arts Centre](https://www.chapter.cymru/) and **Riverfront Theatre**. He played Sydney Curnow Vosper in _[Salem](https://www.shermantheatre.co.uk/event/salem/)_, commissioned by [Sherman Theatre](https://www.shermantheatre.co.uk/), and is keen to continue working with companies and artists across the UK and beyond.

Alongside performing, Reilly develops his own work, including an original one-act play **_Write of Passage_**. He is a passionate Welsh learner.
