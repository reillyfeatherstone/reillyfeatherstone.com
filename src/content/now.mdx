## Learning Welsh

As someone that went to an English Language Welsh school, I was always dismissive of the language and paid no attention in Welsh classes. But at the age of 18 I moved away from my four walls and realised what I was missing, how valuable the language is, and that there’s a very real community that comes with it. I’m now spending a couple of hours a week learning Welsh, currently at a B1 level on Dysgu Cymraeg’s Canolradd course, along with using Anki flash cards and doing as much immersion as I can get.

My long-term goal is to work on a Welsh language TV Show/Film.
